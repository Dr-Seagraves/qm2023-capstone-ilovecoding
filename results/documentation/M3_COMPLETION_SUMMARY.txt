INTERPRETATION:
  • Leverage (debt-to-assets) shows modest positive effects on returns,
    lag 2 marginally  significant
  • BETA (systematic risk) is HIGHLY SIGNIFICANT: 1-unit increase in beta
    increases monthly return by 61 basis points (≈7.3% annualized)
  • Result consistent with CAPM risk premium theory

Conclusion: NOT STATISTICALLY SIGNIFICANT – Large REITs show no differential
